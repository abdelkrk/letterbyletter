PER-FRAME TIMING METHOD
qwen_audio_tts does not return word-level timestamps, and forced alignment (ASR)
was judged less reliable than a direct approach given Arabic MSA. Each chunk's
TOTAL duration was measured exactly via ffprobe (not estimated). Within a chunk,
each frame's slice was apportioned in proportion to its Arabic word count relative
to the chunk's total word count. This is a standard pseudo-alignment technique;
the total (chunk-level) timing is exact, only the internal split between
consecutive frames within one chunk is word-count-proportional rather than
individually measured. See frame_timing.csv for the resulting start/end per frame.
