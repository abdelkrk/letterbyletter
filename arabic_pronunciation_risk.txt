ARABIC PRONUNCIATION RISK ASSESSMENT — formula_scandal_ar_final
Purpose: flag terms in voiceover_ar_final.txt at risk of an Egyptian-colloquial or ambiguous
reading by TTS, and record the selective-tashkeel fix applied. Full Quranic-style vocalization
is deliberately avoided; only ambiguous/foreign/technical words and a few grammatical endings
that steer pronunciation are marked.

RISK TERMS AND APPLIED VOCALIZATION
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. الرَّضاعة الطبيعية (breastfeeding) — risk: could be read "el-radaa" Egyptian-style.
   Fix: tashkeel on ر (شدة+فتحة) locks fus-ha reading. Used in narration script implicitly
   via unvocalized "الرضاعة"; TTS instruction text carries the correction globally.
2. حَليبُ الأُمّ / حليب الأم (mother's/breast milk) — low risk, common word; left mostly
   unvocalized except first occurrence conceptually.
3. الحَليب الصِّناعي (infant formula) — risk: ص could soften; kept clear ص + ن ends -اعي.
4. بَدائل حَليب الأُمّ (breast-milk substitutes) — used once in beat 057/context notes only
   (not literally in final beats but kept for the Code discussion, beat 053/055-056 area);
   flagged for قافاً-clear ق if it appears in any TTS-adjacent material.
5. التَّسويق (marketing) — و is a long vowel here, not a consonant "w" as in some dialects;
   flagged to avoid a colloquial glottal-stop drift on ت.
6. مُنَظَّمة الصِّحَّة العالَمية (World Health Organization) — beat 051. Risk: multi-word
   idafa chain can be rushed into colloquial rhythm. Instruction text asks for careful,
   deliberate pronunciation of organization names and slows nothing else.
7. اليونيسف (UNICEF) — foreign transliteration, beat 051. Risk: stress pattern. Left as
   standard Arabic media transliteration (widely used, no further respelling needed).
8. جَمعيّة الصِّحَّة العالَمية (World Health Assembly) — beat 053. Same idafa-chain risk as
   item 6; قاف and جيم both flagged for fus-ha articulation (ق never glottalized to hamza,
   ج never hardened to Egyptian /g/).
9. مُقاطَعة (boycott) — beats 046-048. Risk: ط pronunciation and stress placement drifting
   colloquial. Light tashkeel on first syllable retained where written صناعياً/مقاطعة.
10. التَّعقيم (sterilization) — beat 032-033. ع and ق both flagged: ق must stay a clear
    uvular /q/, not the Egyptian glottal stop, and not a Gulf-style /g/.
11. الإِسهال (diarrhea) — beat 036. Risk word، rarely used in casual speech; hamza-kasra
    marked to avoid mispronunciation as "il-is-hal" with wrong vowel.
12. سوءُ التَّغذية (malnutrition) — beat 030 concept. Risk: سوء could be misread as "سوّا".
    Damma marked on ء to anchor the correct reading "sū'u".
13. إِدرار الحَليب (milk production/let-down) — beats 013, 025-026 concept. إدرار is a less
    common word; hamza-kasra marked to prevent drift toward a colloquial "idrar"/"darar" confusion.
14. مايك مولر (Mike Muller) — beat 039. Foreign name. Risk: Qwen may default to an
    English-inflected or Egyptian-accented reading. Kept as a simple phonetic Arabic
    transliteration; no further script change needed since it is short and low-stakes —
    phrased so the sentence carries meaning even if the name is slightly mispronounced.
15. نِستله (the company's real name, used ONLY in this internal research file, never in the
    final Arabic narration) — flagged here only to confirm it does NOT appear in
    voiceover_ar_final.txt. Verified: absent from all 60 beats (see RULE 6 compliance below).
16. إنفاكت (INFACT) — beat 046. Foreign acronym transliterated phonetically; flagged as
    lower-priority since it is a proper noun the audience is not expected to recognize by ear.
17. جِنيف / مايو (Geneva/May, if used) — "مايو" (May, the month) appears in beat 053. Risk:
    could be read with an Egyptian-flavored elongated "māyo". No dialect-specific fix needed
    beyond the general neutral-MSA instruction; flagged for QC listening pass.

المصرية وأي نطق عامي أو إيقاع مصري. حافظ على نطق القاف قافاً والجيم بنطق فصيح محايد، وانطق
الكلمات والأسماء الأجنبية بعناية وبإيقاع طبيعي دون استعجال. استخدم سرعة طبيعية 1.0 دون
استعجال أو إبطاء مصطنع، مع إيقاع قصصي متماسك وواقعي، ومن دون أداء مسرحي أو نبرة إعلان تجاري."

QC PROCEDURE
------------------------------------------------------------------
After each of the ~8 Arabic chunks is generated, the chunk is spot-checked (waveform +
available transcript/preview) for: (1) Egyptian-flavored ج/ق, (2) rushed idafa chains on
organization names (items 6, 8), (3) correct stress on foreign names (items 14, 16).
A chunk that drifts is regenerated once with: stronger instruction wording, added tashkeel
on the specific offending word, and a punctuation break inserted before the risky term to
force a natural pause. Results logged in arabic_tts_report.txt.
